About this home

Two homes, one address

A vacant two-story duplex on a corner lot in Richmond's Belding Woods neighborhood, with a separate entrance for each unit, an attached garage, and gated parking. Vacant duplexes like this are rare to find in Richmond.

The upper unit is reached by its own private staircase. It has three bedrooms and two full baths, a bright living room with a nook that works as a home office, and a kitchen with granite counters, white cabinets, a stainless double-oven range, recessed lighting, and a tall built-in pantry shelf.

The primary bedroom is a large corner room with windows on two sides, a big closet, and its own bathroom. The other two bedrooms both have sliding glass doors to the balcony. A second full bath and a laundry room finish the unit.

The lower unit has fresh white paint and plank flooring throughout, a long open living room, and an updated kitchen with quartzite counters, stainless appliances, a dishwasher, a French-door refrigerator, and a skylight. There are two bedrooms, a bonus room with its own door to the outside, an updated bathroom with a tiled tub surround and matte black fixtures, a second full bath with a double-sink vanity, extra storage space, and its own laundry room.

Since 2022 the owners have put about $82,000 into upgrades. Both units have new flooring, fresh interior paint, new water heaters, and new stoves, refrigerators, and washers and dryers. The lower unit also has new windows and a remodeled bathroom with a new tub and sink. Outside, the building has fresh exterior paint, replaced exterior wood, a new back fence, and a new garage door.

Outside, the yard is fully fenced, and there's easy freeway access. Both units are vacant, so you can move into one and rent out the other, or set your own rents on both from day one.
